## Problem Description
In colleges, universities, public places, and institutions, people frequently lose personal belongings such as mobile phones, wallets, ID cards, keys, bags, books, and other valuable items.

Currently, finding these items is difficult because there is no centralized platform where people can report missing belongings and search for items that have been found by others.

Traditional methods such as informing security guards, contacting administration, or sharing messages in WhatsApp groups are often inefficient and time-consuming.

Our proposed solution is a centralized digital Lost & Found platform that connects people who have lost their belongings with those who have found them.
## Proposed Solution
We propose developing an AI-powered website called Campus Lost & Found AI.

The platform allows users to:

Register and log in to their accounts.

Report lost items by providing the item name, description, category, location, date, and image.
Report items they have found.
Search for missing belongings using keywords and filters.
Upload images of lost or found items.
Receive AI-generated suggestions for potentially matching lost and found items.
Contact the relevant person or administrator through the platform to verify and recover the item.

The platform eliminates the need to depend entirely on manual announcements, notice boards, and scattered WhatsApp messages.
It brings all lost and found reports into one centralized system, making it easier to search, identify, and recover missing belongings.

## Feasibility and Viability
1. Analysis of the Feasibility of the Idea

The proposed solution is technically and economically feasible because it can be developed using open-source technologies and commonly available development tools.
The initial version can be implemented as a web application without requiring expensive hardware or infrastructure.
The platform can initially be tested within a college campus and later expanded to universities, offices, public institutions, and other locations.

2. Potential Challenges and Risks
Incorrect matches generated by the AI system.
Fake or misleading lost and found reports.
Difficulty identifying items with incomplete descriptions.
Privacy concerns involving personal belongings and user information.
Maintaining accurate and updated item records.
3. Strategies for Overcoming These Challenges

Use AI similarity scores to rank possible matches rather than automatically confirming them.
Introduce administrator verification for suspicious reports.
Allow users to upload images and provide detailed descriptions.
Implement secure login and access controls.
Provide reporting and removal options for false or resolved entries.
Ensure that personal contact details are not publicly exposed.
