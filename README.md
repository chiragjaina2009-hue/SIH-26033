## Problem Descriptionn the agricultural sector, farmers often depend on multiple intermediaries, including local traders, wholesalers, distributors and retailers, to sell their agricultural products.

Due to the involvement of several intermediaries, farmers frequently receive lower prices for their produce, while consumers are forced to pay significantly higher prices.

Small-scale farmers also face difficulties in accessing larger markets, identifying potential buyers, understanding current market prices and arranging affordable transportation.

Additionally, the absence of a transparent supply-chain system makes it difficult to track agricultural products, coordinate demand and reduce unnecessary transportation and storage costs.

Traditional agricultural marketing systems often lack proper coordination between farmers and buyers, resulting in inefficiencies, price differences and unnecessary wastage of agricultural produce.

Therefore, there is a need for an intelligent digital platform that connects farmers directly with buyers, improves price transparency, combines agricultural produce from multiple farmers and optimizes the overall supply chain.

Proposed Solution

We propose developing an intelligent agricultural platform called AGRISEVA – A Demand-Driven, Farmer-Centric Agricultural Supply Network.

AGRISEVA aims to transform the traditional agricultural supply chain by connecting farmers directly with consumers, retailers, restaurants, supermarkets and bulk buyers.

Unlike ordinary agricultural marketplace applications, which mainly allow farmers to upload their products and wait for customers, AGRISEVA follows a demand-first approach.

Buyers can publish their agricultural requirements, including the product, required quantity, preferred price range and delivery location. The platform then identifies suitable farmers based on their available produce, location, quantity and pricing.

The platform also introduces a farmer-pooling mechanism, where multiple nearby small-scale farmers can combine their agricultural produce to fulfil larger orders.

Major Features of AGRISEVA

Farmer Registration and Product Listing:

Farmers can register, create profiles and list their agricultural products, available quantities, expected prices, harvest dates and locations.

Demand-First Buyer System:

Consumers, retailers, restaurants and bulk buyers can publish their requirements before purchasing. The system identifies suitable farmers who can fulfil these requirements.

AI-Based Farmer and Buyer Matching:

An intelligent matching system recommends suitable farmers and agricultural products based on location, quantity, price, availability and buyer requirements.

Farmer Pooling Mechanism:

Small-scale farmers located in nearby areas can combine their produce to fulfil bulk orders, helping them access larger markets without individually managing large orders.

Transparent Price Calculation:

Farmers and buyers can view the estimated price breakdown, including the farmer's expected earnings, transportation expenses, platform service charges and final buyer price.

Smart Collection and Distribution Centres:

The platform identifies nearby collection points where farmers can bring their produce. Combined orders can then be transported to buyers through coordinated logistics.

AI-Based Demand Forecasting:

The system analyses previous orders, buyer requirements and available market information to estimate future demand for agricultural products.

Order Tracking and Digital Communication:

Farmers and buyers can track order status, communicate through the platform and monitor the delivery process.

The main objective of AGRISEVA is to reduce unnecessary intermediaries, improve farmers' earnings, provide fairer prices to consumers and create a more transparent agricultural supply chain.
The AGRISEVA platform will operate through the following process:

Step 1: User Registration

Farmers, consumers, retailers and bulk buyers register on the platform using their respective accounts.

Step 2: Farmer Product Registration

Farmers enter their agricultural product details, including crop name, quantity, expected price, harvest date and location.

Step 3: Buyer Requirement Submission

Buyers publish their requirements by specifying the agricultural product, quantity, preferred price range and delivery location.

Step 4: Intelligent Matching System

The platform analyses buyer requirements and available farmer listings to identify suitable matches.

Step 5: Farmer Pooling and Order Aggregation

If one farmer cannot fulfil the entire requirement, the platform identifies nearby farmers who have the same agricultural product and combines their available quantities.

Step 6: Transparent Price Calculation

The system calculates an estimated final price by considering the farmer's selling price, transportation expenses and platform charges.

Step 7: Order Confirmation

The buyer reviews the available products, prices and delivery details before confirming the order. Farmers receive the order request and can accept or reject it.

Step 8: Collection and Transportation

Farmers deliver their produce to an identified collection point. The combined produce is prepared for transportation to the buyer.

Step 9: Delivery and Payment

The buyer receives the agricultural products, and the payment is processed through the selected payment method.

Step 10: Feedback and Performance Analysis

Farmers and buyers can provide feedback. The platform uses accumulated transaction information to improve future matching and demand predictions.

Innovation and Uniqueness

The main innovation of AGRISEVA is its demand-first agricultural supply-chain model combined with intelligent farmer pooling and transparent price calculation.

Unlike conventional agricultural marketplace platforms that mainly focus on displaying agricultural products, AGRISEVA focuses on coordinating agricultural demand, supply, aggregation and delivery.

Key Innovative Features

1. Demand-First Agricultural Marketplace

Instead of depending only on farmers uploading their products, buyers can first publish their requirements. The platform then identifies farmers who can fulfil those requirements.

2. Intelligent Farmer Pooling

The platform identifies nearby small-scale farmers growing the same agricultural products and combines their available quantities to fulfil larger orders.

This allows farmers who individually have limited quantities to participate in bulk-market opportunities.

3. Transparent Price Distribution System

The platform displays a clear price breakdown showing the farmer's expected earnings, transportation expenses, service charges and final buyer price.

This improves transparency and helps both parties make informed decisions.

4. AI-Based Demand Forecasting

Machine learning models analyse historical orders and demand patterns to estimate future agricultural requirements.

This can help farmers and buyers plan their agricultural transactions more effectively.

5. Smart Collection Centre Allocation

The system identifies suitable collection points based on farmer locations, order quantities and buyer destinations.

6. Shared Logistics Coordination

Instead of arranging separate transportation for every farmer, the platform coordinates combined deliveries wherever practical, helping reduce transportation costs.

7. Multilingual and Mobile-Friendly Interface

The platform can support regional languages, including Tamil and English, making it more accessible to farmers who may not be comfortable using complex digital applications.

The system will use a modular architecture consisting of farmer management, buyer management, product listings, demand matching, farmer aggregation, price calculation, order management and logistics coordination.

## Feasibility and Viability
. Analysis of the Feasibility of the Idea

AGRISEVA is technically and economically feasible because it can be developed using open-source programming languages, frameworks and database technologies.

The initial version can be developed as a web application without requiring expensive hardware or infrastructure.

The project can initially be tested with a small group of farmers, local vegetable retailers and nearby consumers.

Instead of immediately establishing physical warehouses, the initial implementation can coordinate orders through existing local collection points and agricultural collection facilities.

The AI-based matching system can initially use rule-based matching and basic machine learning techniques. More advanced demand forecasting and logistics optimization can be introduced as sufficient transaction data becomes available.

The platform can gradually expand to different agricultural products, markets and geographical regions.

2. Potential Challenges and Risks

Farmers may have limited access to smartphones or reliable internet connectivity.

Inaccurate product quantities or quality information may affect order fulfilment.

Farmers may not always be able to fulfil large buyer requirements individually.

Transportation and collection expenses may reduce the expected financial benefits.

Agricultural prices may fluctuate depending on demand, supply and seasonal conditions.

Buyers and farmers may hesitate to adopt a new digital platform.

AI-generated demand predictions may not always be accurate.

Maintaining trust, product quality and timely delivery may be challenging.

3. Strategies for Overcoming These Challenges

Develop a simple, mobile-friendly and multilingual interface with Tamil language support.

Introduce product verification and quantity confirmation before finalizing orders.

Implement the farmer-pooling mechanism to combine produce from multiple nearby farmers.

Use existing collection points and shared transportation wherever possible.

Display updated market information and transparent price breakdowns.

Provide simple registration and onboarding support for farmers.

Use AI predictions as recommendations rather than guaranteed future prices or demand.

Introduce order tracking, feedback and verification mechanisms to improve reliability.
